# 12 Week Build Plan

## Week 1

Supabase schema, RLS, auth profile bootstrap, client scaffolds, and environment examples.

## Week 2

Shared design language on Android and web: typography, colors, surfaces, buttons, cards, inputs, empty states, and skeletons.

## Week 3

Accounts, categories, transactions CRUD, realtime subscriptions, and optimistic write patterns.

## Week 4

Dashboard, charts, recent activity, insights preview, and net worth summaries.

## Week 5

Statement import with client-side CSV/PDF parsing, column mapping, preview, confirmation, and server fallback.

## Week 6

Categorization Edge Function, rule engine, learned mappings, Groq fallback, Gemini fallback, and AI activity logging.

## Week 7

Receipt OCR, Android SMS parsing review queue, and web OCR fallback.

## Week 8

Budgets, goals, subscriptions, reminders, and recurring detection.

## Week 9

Debts, investments, net worth snapshots, and payoff calculators.

## Week 10

Scheduled insights generation, feed UI, and action detail screens.

## Week 11

Reports, exports, offline queue hardening, and sync conflict coverage.

## Week 12

Accessibility, motion polish, release signing, Vercel deployment, and Play Store readiness.
