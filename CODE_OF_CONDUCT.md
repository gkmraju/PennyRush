# Code Of Conduct

Pennyrush should be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent while still addressing problems clearly.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussion focused on the work and the people affected by it.
- Be especially careful with privacy and security topics.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Dismissing security or privacy concerns without investigation.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Reporting

Use GitHub issues or repository maintainers to report conduct concerns. For security-sensitive issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and avoid posting private details publicly.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project healthy.
